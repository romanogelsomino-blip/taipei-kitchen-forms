Taipei Kitchen Bento Operations

Production and delivery logging for the Giant bento program

Taipei Kitchen cooks ready-to-eat bento for Giant supermarkets across central Pennsylvania. This system logs every batch from kitchen to shelf: cook and cooling times when the food is made, then cooler temperatures, stock counts, and case photos at each store delivery.

Every entry lands in the operations spreadsheet, where it backs HACCP food-safety compliance and feeds the live dashboard.
