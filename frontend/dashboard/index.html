Taipei Kitchen Bento Operations

Production and delivery logging for the Giant bento program

What this system does

Taipei Kitchen cooks ready-to-eat bento for Giant supermarkets across central Pennsylvania. Every batch is logged from kitchen to shelf: cook and cooling times when the food is made, then cooler temperatures, stock counts, and case photos at each store delivery.

Every entry lands in the operations spreadsheet, where it backs HACCP food-safety compliance and feeds this dashboard. The sections in the menu show what has been logged.

Production Log

Kitchen staff record each batch: cook and cooling times, the final temperature, and how much of each dish was made or discarded.

Delivery Form

Drivers record each store drop: product and cooler temperatures, what was stocked and pulled, and case photos.

Store QR codes

The code posted at a store opens the delivery form with that store already filled in. Generate one here to replace a code that has been damaged or lost.
